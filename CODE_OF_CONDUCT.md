# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the KB-1 community a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and
orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas, behavior, and code rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove sensitive data from public reports.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces, including repository discussions, issues, pull
requests, events, and project-related communication.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@metatheory.dev` with the subject
`KB-1 Code of Conduct`. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

Project maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, protect the
reporter's privacy as far as practical, and may edit or remove contributions,
comments, commits, code, issues, or other material that violates this policy.
They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
